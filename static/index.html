Prototype coverage and hardware qualification

Implemented

Station interlocks; driver supply and logical fiber timing; static gate, blocking, conduction, diode and insulation checks; both switch positions; double-pulse switching; explicit companion-fault protection; estimated thermal response; discharge and residual-energy verification; complete report and raw traces.

Electrical calculations drive the 3D cursor. Cabinet CAD is a concept layout; it does not calculate field distribution, mechanical stress or insulation clearance.

Required for actual-module qualification

    The public surrogate, prototype limits and assumed thermal/driver models are identified in every report. Missing evidence is reported as incomplete.
